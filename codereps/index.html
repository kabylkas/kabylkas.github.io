Methodology · the practice is the exam

  1. Exam tickets. Professors generate a pool, say 30 problems for the week. The quiz draws 5 of them at random.
  2. 100% aligned. Quiz problems are the ones students practised, so they know exactly what to study and try to solve them all.
  3. Metacognition. With chatbots, students overrate what they know (ICER 2024). Here they do the thinking, and hidden tests show what they really know.
  4. Their pedagogy. Professors steer the AI with their own instructions: scaffolding, common mistakes, the framing they teach with.
